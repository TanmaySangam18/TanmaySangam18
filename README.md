### How I work

- **Find the moment someone gives up.** Not wireframes first — the abandoned form, the fare gate they route around.
- **Design the incentive, not the enforcement.** Friction produces avoidance. The MBTA had a $25M enforcement problem; I built the rewarding path instead.
- **Restraint is a feature.** What a product refuses to show carries more weight than most layouts.
- **Ship it, then watch where people hesitate.** Rough builds in real hands beat polished mocks.
